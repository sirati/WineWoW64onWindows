Remaining runtime work

The Civ3 target adds two active areas to the earlier headless baseline. D/F
native-thread/window behavior is validated in Luna's logged-on interactive
session. The remaining Civ3 work is the first smoke launch and Wine's own
headless GUI path: win32u, wow64win, wineserver window/message state and the
existing null display driver. Guest user32/gdi32 must use that Wine path; the
host's win32u is not a substitute. Sound output and ARM64 remain excluded.

The accepted baseline and focused results are recorded in README.txt and
the Luna evidence bundles. The demonstrated forced-termination wait races
pass on 5c02e38. This table distinguishes verified controls from remaining gaps.
Difficulty is for this prototype, not for full Wine.

Upstream test failures unrelated to GUI or excluded NT services take priority.
The current per-suite results and easiest fixes are in docs/upstream-wine-tests.txt.
That report includes native controls, rejected test runs and the source of each
failure classification. The original upstream fiber suite now completes with
only its excluded subprocess failures. Static TLS and the shared-clock-page
query also have verified fixes. Alignment-exception dispatch and the remaining
memory-accounting differences still require work.
The protected-syscall wrapper passes native plus ten Wine conversion-fault
controls, with a corrected prior-runtime crash control. The full thread suite
now reaches later failures and a timeout. The group-affinity conversion passes
native plus ten Wine controls on cd13e0f, with five prior-runtime failures.
The legacy pagefile fixture passes on an 8 GiB VM where unsigned addition
bypasses Wine's Photoshop clamp, and fails all five Wine runs on 2 GiB with
the exact clamp formula. Native passes. Old-binary controls remain pending.
The T3 project and guest-access blockers are resolved. The incremental runner
closes the earlier lost-suite-exit gap with twenty-two child exits 0 and 139
fully identified process records. A wake-all versus wake-single diagnostic
isolates the missing batched-alert wake route, without completing the whole
upstream thread suite or adding a guest service. See the upstream report.
